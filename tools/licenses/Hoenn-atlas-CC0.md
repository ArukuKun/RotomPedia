The image is a community recreation with English labels, **not official or final
cartography**. Section hit areas use a separately documented approximate affine
calibration (`HoennAtlasProjection`), inspected against town centres. Selection
of overlapping sections always offers all candidates. Full spatial QA on a
phone, finer coastal/water-route polygons and Spanish labelling remain pending.
The list of audited areas is the accessible, exact-ID alternative. Geometry
never implies encounter/item availability; the verified Emerald catalogue is
unchanged. Old regional images have no newly established licence by association.
